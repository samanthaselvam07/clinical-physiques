Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Clinical Physiques ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your first name, email address and resource preferences when you join our resource list.
  • Technical information: your IP address and browser type, recorded with form submissions to help prevent spam and abuse.
  • Coaching applications: applications are submitted through our coaching platform, CompleteCoach, and handled under its privacy terms as well as this policy.

How we use it

  • To send the resources and educational updates you asked for.
  • To respond to your enquiries and coaching applications.
  • To protect our forms from spam and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run this website: Vercel (hosting), Neon (database storage) and Resend (email delivery). They process your information only to provide these services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you remain on our list or are a client, and delete them on request.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time, and you can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@clinicalphysiques.com.

Health information

Coaching is not medical care. Any health information you share during coaching is used only to personalise your program and is kept confidential.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@clinicalphysiques.com.
