Why Clinical Physiques?

You do not need more information. You need a method you trust.

Most of our clients already know the basics. They have tried plans, tracked macros and started over more times than they would like to admit. What was missing was never execution.

It was understanding. Why the plan is built the way it is, how to read progress without spiralling, and what to change when life gets in the way.

So we coach the decisions behind the outcome, not just the outcome. You end up with a deeper knowledge about your health and have built lifelong habits.

Clinically informed

Health history, recovery, biofeedback and bloodwork shape your plan, so decisions are made from facts rather than guesswork.

Built to be executed

Training and nutrition that hold up through work, family, travel and social life, with minimum standards for the busy weeks.

Education first

Every adjustment comes with the reasoning behind it. You learn the principles, so progress does not depend on us forever.

The Clinical Physiques Method

Assess. Execute. Review. Refine.

A simple loop, run with precision every week. It is how we find the real bottleneck and keep you progressing long after motivation fades.

  1. Assess

    Nutrition, training, recovery, stress, digestion, routine and bloodwork are reviewed together, so the plan matches the actual problem.

  2. Execute

    A structured plan with clear priorities and minimum standards, flexible enough to survive real life.

  3. Review

    Weekly check-ins look at body composition, photos, performance and biofeedback, in context with a calm clinical lens.

  4. Refine

    Evidence-based adjustments, explained clearly, so every change teaches you something you keep.

Questions

Before you apply.

Anything else? Email hello@clinicalphysiques.com

Who is coaching for?

Men and women who want to improve body composition, health and training performance, and who want to understand the process rather than follow it blindly. Most clients already know the basics but struggle to stay consistent when life gets busy.

How much does coaching cost?

Investment depends on the model (online or hybrid) and the level of support you need. We share exact pricing on your consultation call, so you can decide with all the details and no pressure.

What does "bloodwork-informed" mean?

If you have recent blood tests, we use them as context alongside recovery, energy, digestion and performance when making nutrition and training decisions. We are not a medical service and will always refer you to a doctor for medical concerns.

Do I need gym experience?

No. Hybrid coaching is ideal if you are newer to structured strength training, because in-person sessions build technique and confidence quickly. Online coaching suits people who already train consistently.

How often will I hear from my coach?

You will have a structured weekly check-in with clear adjustments and the reasoning behind them, so you always know what to focus on next.

What happens after I apply?

We personally review every application. If it looks like a good fit, we will be in touch to book a consultation call. If not, we will point you toward resources that can help.
